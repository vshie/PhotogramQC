PhotogramQC for macOS (Apple Silicon)

macOS Gatekeeper blocks apps that are not signed by an Apple Developer ID.
Use one of these the first time you open it:

1. Right-click PhotogramQC.app, choose Open, then click Open again.
2. Or in Terminal, from the folder that contains the app:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine PhotogramQC.app
   open PhotogramQC.app

Then pick the folder that contains the survey pictures.
Close the PhotogramQC window when you are finished (that stops the server).
